Slide 3: Weather App Core Features
---------------------------------
Let’s look at some key features of the Weather App:

Current Weather Info – It provides up-to-date information on temperature, humidity, wind speed, and weather conditions.
Hourly Forecast – Users can check the weather for the upcoming hours, giving them a detailed breakdown.
7-Day Forecast – This feature allows users to see weather predictions for the next week.
City Search – Users can search for any city and view its weather information instantly.


Slide 4: Weather App - Technology Stack:
---------------------------------
We used Flutter for building a cross-platform app, integrating it with WeatherAPI for real-time weather data. Dart powers the app, and Material Design ensures a clean and modern user interface.



Slide 5: Home Page - Display Today Weather
---------------------------------
We See the home page is designed to give users a quick overview of today’s weather. It shows essential information like the location, temperature, condition, wind speed, and humidity. The page also includes buttons for viewing the hourly and 7-day forecasts and also search functionality for city weather, making it easy for users to navigate through the app for detailed weather insights.



Slide 6: Search Box
---------------------------------
The app features a search box where users can enter any city name. Once a city is entered, the app fetches the weather data for that city and displays it on a new page, showing details like temperature, condition, wind speed, and humidity, just like the home page.



Slide 7: Navigation Button - Hourly Forecast
---------------------------------
The 'Hourly Forecast' button on the home page allows users to access detailed weather updates, including temperature, wind speed, and more.



Slide 8: Navigation Button - 7 day Forecast

On the home page, there's a button labeled '7-Day Forecast.' When clicked, it navigates users to a detailed view of the upcoming week’s weather including date, condition, max & minimum temperature.
